Superconductors above 77 K

Can composition features help identify known superconductors whose critical temperature exceeds the liquid-nitrogen threshold?

Seven systems evaluated on the same 4,253 final records. Research results, not a material certification.

Random Forest led this comparison

It reached 90.4% balanced accuracy. The three-model eLCS ensemble performed worse than its matched single model, despite using more training effort.

Balanced accuracy gives equal weight to finding above-77-K and at-or-below-77-K records. It is not the percentage of all records classified correctly.

How the models compared

Final balanced accuracy, higher is better. All bars use the same 0–100% scale.

The ensemble did not help

4.8 percentage points lower balanced accuracy

The corrected single eLCS scored 63.1%; the corrected ensemble scored 58.3%. The ensemble combined three seeds by majority vote.

What changed in practice?

Of 779 above-77-K records, the ensemble detected fewer and missed more. It reduced false positives at the cost of recall.

Recall fell from 36.2% to 21.4%. Precision rose from 45.0% to 50.0%. This is a trade-off, not an improvement on the declared primary metric.

How certain is that difference?

The predeclared paired test found a statistically significant difference against the ensemble: p = 0.00005, using a two-sided 5% significance threshold.

Whole identical-feature groups were swapped together, preserving duplicate dependence. The test used 19,999 random swaps, seed 42, across 3,031 groups (328 with nonzero effects).

The p-value uses a plus-one correction and is the smallest reportable value at this sampling budget. It is not the probability that the result is wrong. The test assumes independent groups and interchangeable model predictions under the null; unknown material-family relationships may violate those assumptions.

Open the statistical record

Each eLCS member used 1,000 iterations and population limit 100. Three members mean three times the single model's total iteration budget. No settings changed after final scores were viewed.

Read the scores without the jargon

All metrics below use final records, not training results. Precision, recall and F1 treat above 77 K as the positive class.

What does each metric mean?
Balanced accuracy
The average of recall for the two classes. Each class has equal importance, even though above-77-K records are less common. The primary comparison metric.
Precision
Of records predicted to be above 77 K, how many actually were? Higher precision means fewer false leads.
Recall
Of records actually above 77 K, how many did the model find? Higher recall means fewer missed candidates.
F1
The harmonic mean of positive-class precision and recall. It balances these two quantities but does not include true negatives.

What the learned rules say

These are three real rules from the final ensemble, one per seed. Each rule describes a combination of feature conditions and votes for one class.

Within a member, all matching rules vote using fitness multiplied by numerosity. The ensemble then takes the majority of the three member predictions. A single sample rule does not determine the final answer.

If the rules cannot produce a useful decision, the corrected library falls back to the majority training class. That fallback is not a rule-based explanation.

These rules have very few training matches. They are inspectable model patterns, not physical laws or trustworthy standalone recommendations.

Where the evidence stops

These findings apply to known superconductors in this dataset. A prediction does not establish that an arbitrary material is superconducting or commercially useful.

  • The final records had appeared in historical full-data analyses and rule exports. They are not a retroactively untouched external dataset.
  • Identical feature vectors were grouped together, but related compositions and material families may still cross partitions.
  • Only a bounded set of eLCS budgets was tested. The study does not establish convergence or the best achievable performance of the algorithm.
  • The library repair and the ensemble enhancement are separate changes. The ensemble's negative result must not be credited as an improvement.
  • Model predictions are screening signals. Physical feasibility, operating margin, current, field response, cost and experimental validation remain outside this evaluation.
